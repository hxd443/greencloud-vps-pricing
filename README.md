## The fine print worth knowing before you check out

This is where coupon sites go quiet, and where a code can turn into a bad deal if you don't know the rules.

**Refunds are narrow.** New customers can get a refund on their first VPS within 7 days. That's it. Plans sold through sale pages are marked "No refund/Money back on this plan" directly in the cart, and discounted plans generally fall outside the refund policy. If you're buying specifically to use a promo code on a sale plan, treat the purchase as final.

**Payments and verification.** GreenCloud accepts PayPal, credit/debit cards, Alipay, UnionPay, crypto (BTC/ETH/BNB/USDT and others), and US/UK/EU bank transfers. Two wrinkles: new customers paying by card or PayPal go through a one-time verification per payment method, and community guides consistently report that brand-new accounts ordering through a proxy with card or PayPal tend to trigger KYC requests — Alipay tends to sail through. Inaccurate billing details can also delay provisioning through the anti-fraud system.

**It's unmanaged, with strict CPU limits.** Support covers the physical server and uptime, not your software. The official pages self-report an average ticket response of 9 minutes (the FAQ elsewhere says 12 — the company's own numbers disagree). Community feedback on the CPU limits is blunt: they're enforced. If your workload is CPU-sustained rather than bursty, the dedicated cores on the EPYC VDS line are the safer match, and EPYCVDS-1 at $10/month with a dedicated core and 8GB RAM undercuts what most budget shared-core plans give you at that price.

**Provisioning and stock.** Standard setup is advertised within 15 minutes, up to 2 hours in edge cases. Popular locations sell out — during a recent check, 12 of 15 listed Budget KVM locations showed zero stock. If a specific city matters to you, check availability before committing to a billing cycle.
